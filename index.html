ABOUT ME

The Dark Hero
Has Arrived

I'm Sawon Islam — a developer, creator, and die-hard anime fan who lives at the intersection of code and culture. I don't just build websites; I craft digital experiences with the same care a mangaka gives to their panels.

My path isn't the conventional one. I picked up coding not because it was expected, but because I wanted to build my own world — and this is how I do it. Every project is a new arc, every bug is a boss fight, every shipped feature is a power-up.

"A hero doesn't need approval. The arc is already decided — rise, fall, and rise again stronger."

When I'm not writing code, I'm watching anime at 3am, grinding ranked matches, designing content, or absorbing everything the internet has to offer. Student by day. Builder by night. Otaku always.
